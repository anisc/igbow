Privacy Policy

Effective date:

Igbow Games ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile applications we publish on the Apple App Store ("the Apps") handle your information.

Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed to be simple and fun. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. Our Apps do not require you to create an account and do not request access to your contacts, photos, location, or other personal data on your device.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

Our Apps do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data about your usage, device, or session is transmitted to us or to any third party.

In-App Purchases

If the Apps offer in-app purchases, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Any payment information is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we never receive or store your payment details.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or from anyone else using the Apps.

Data Security

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information stored on our servers or stored by us at all. All data within the Apps stays locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
